## About Me

Hello! I am Hye-Yoon Baek, an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Konkuk University, advised by Prof. Byungkook Oh in the Graph & Language Intelligence Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of graph learning and large language models. I am particularly interested in information extraction, and in making LLMs more logically consistent by integrating symbolic rules and structured knowledge into their reasoning.
